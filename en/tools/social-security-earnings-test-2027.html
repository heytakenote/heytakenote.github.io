How withholding works

  • SSA usually holds back whole monthly checks from the start of the year until the amount owed is covered. That's why the calculator shows full months.
  • If the whole months add up to more than required, the extra is paid back the following year.
  • Withheld benefits aren't lost for good. At FRA, SSA recalculates your monthly benefit to give credit for the months it held back.
  • In your first year of retirement, a special monthly rule can pay a full check for any month your wages stay under the monthly limit, whatever you earned for the year.

FAQ

Does my spouse's income count?
No. Only your own earnings from work count toward your limit. But withholding from your record can reduce benefits paid to family members on it.

What if my estimate changes?
Report updated earnings estimates to SSA during the year to avoid overpayments you'd later have to repay.
