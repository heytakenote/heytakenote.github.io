Saver's Match Calculator 2027

Starting with contributions made in 2027, the Saver's Match replaces the Saver's Credit for retirement contributions. The government deposits up to $1,000 straight into your retirement account. Values as of 2026-11-27.

How the match is calculated

  • The match rate starts at 50% of up to $2,000 of contributions per person, so the most anyone gets is $1,000.
  • Above the income threshold, the rate drops: reduction = 50 points × (MAGI − threshold) ÷ phase-out range, rounded down to a whole point.
  • Contributions are first reduced by distributions you took in the testing period.

Who qualifies

  • Age 18 or older by the end of 2027.
  • Not a full-time student and not claimed as someone else's dependent.
  • Nonresident aliens are excluded unless they elect to be treated as residents.

Where the money goes

The match is deposited into an eligible account: a 401(k), 403(b), governmental 457(b), or an IRA that is not a Roth IRA. Roth 401(k) money can't receive it either. You claim it on your 2027 return (filed in 2028) with the new Form 8880-A. If your match is above $0 but under $100, you can choose to take it as a refundable tax credit instead.

FAQ

Does this replace the Saver's Credit?
For retirement plan and IRA contributions, yes, starting in tax year 2027. The Saver's Credit stays for contributions to ABLE accounts.

Do I need to owe tax to get it?
No. Unlike the old credit, the match is paid even if you have no income tax liability.

Is the IRS guidance final?
Not yet. Notice 2026-48 describes intended rules, and proposed regulations are still to come. Check back before you file.
