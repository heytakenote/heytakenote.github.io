Monthly proration vs. the last-month rule

  • Monthly method: you get 1/12 of the annual limit for each month you're eligible on the first day of the month.
  • Last-month rule: if you're eligible on December 1, you can contribute the full-year limit as if you'd been eligible all year.
  • The catch: under the last-month rule you must stay eligible through a testing period (through December 31, 2028 for a 2027 contribution). If you don't, the extra amount becomes taxable income plus a 10% additional tax, unless the reason is death or disability.

FAQ

Married with family coverage?
The $9,000 family limit is shared between spouses. Each spouse 55 or older can add their own $1,000, but only into an HSA in their own name.

What about Medicare?
Once you're enrolled in Medicare, you can't contribute for those months.

Deadline?
You can make 2027 contributions until your 2027 tax return due date (April 2028, not counting extensions).
